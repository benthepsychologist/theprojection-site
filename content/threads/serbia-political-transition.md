## 2026-09-28 — Thread opened; the student movement files its candidate list

- **Serbia's student-led opposition movement, "Students Win," formally filed its candidate list for the October 25 snap parliamentary election, turning President Aleksandar Vučić's Sunday resignation into an actual three-way race between his party, the students, and the pro-Western opposition.** ([ABC News](https://abcnews.com/Business/wireStory/serbias-protesting-youth-formally-join-race-challenging-vucic-136430636))

## 2026-09-27 — Vučić resigns to run for prime minister

- **President Aleksandar Vučić resigned Sunday to run for prime minister in the October 25 snap election, capping nearly two years of anti-corruption protests triggered by the 2024 Novi Sad canopy collapse; parliament speaker Ana Brnabić became acting president.** ([Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2026/09/28/serbias-president-aleksandar-vucic-resigns-to-run-for-prime-minister-in-snap-elections), [France 24](https://www.france24.com/en/europe/20260927-serbian-president-aleksandar-vucic-resigns-expected-to-run-for-prime-minister))
