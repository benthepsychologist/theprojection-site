  tariff talks reportedly broke down, quoted as "they asked too much."
  ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com))
- **Afternoon, same day: Trump put a dated, quantified schedule behind
  it.** He said he will double tariffs on Canadian autos from 25% to
  50%, extend a new 50% tariff to auto parts (previously untariffed),
  and apply 50% to steel — all effective 01 January 2027 — on top of the
  50% tariffs on $20bn of Canadian goods that already took effect the
  same weekend when talks collapsed. Auto executives speaking to Reuters
  were skeptical it lands, noting the January date falls conveniently
  after the November midterms. ([Al
