## 2026-09-11 — Houthis seize Perim Island, completing control of Yemen's entire Red Sea coast and the Bab el-Mandeb strait

- **Houthi forces took Perim (Mayun) Island in an 18-hour push overnight, along with the town of Dhubab and the coastal village of Murad, completing their hold on the whole of Yemen's Red Sea coast and both shipping lanes of the Bab el-Mandeb strait.** A Yemeni government source told Reuters that government forces withdrew from Perim — the island that splits the strait into its two lanes — and from Dhubab; this goes past Thursday's line, which had the advance reaching only the Hanish Islands short of the strait itself. Saudi Arabia answered with two airstrikes on Mocha airport, itself now under Houthi control. For the shipping/oil read on the same advance, see [Red Sea Shock](/threads/red-sea-oil-shock/); for the US-Iran dimension, see [Iran's Widening War](/threads/iran-conflict-widening/). ([Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2026/09/11/houthis-seize-yemeni-island-in-bab-el-mandeb-taking-control-of-the-strait), [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/world/middle-east/houthis-control-bab-el-mandeb-yemen-red-sea-saudi-iran-war-oil-rcna597186), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/11/iran-houthis-mokha-red-sea-yemen.html))
- ⚠️ **Having taken the whole coast, Houthi spokesman Mohammed Abdulsalam said navigation through Bab el-Mandeb is "safe for all" shipping except vessels belonging to Saudi Arabia, describing Houthi operations as defensive and limited to previously announced targets that would stop once strikes on Yemen and the blockade end — the group's own characterisation, not a verified operational fact, and the group has held stretches of this coast before while shipping incidents continued.** Separately, roughly 100 US military personnel are reported in Saudi Arabia sharing intelligence and an AI-assisted targeting tool with Riyadh's own strikes on the Houthis, without US forces themselves striking. ([Times of Israel liveblog](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog-september-11-2026/), [France24](https://www.france24.com/en/middle-east/20260911-middle-east-live-trump-says-he-has-no-regrets-about-launching-war-on-iran))
- ⚠️ **Mohammed bin Salman called Trump twice on Thursday asking for direct US strikes on the Houthis as the coastal collapse unfolded, and Trump refused; US officials say Washington does not currently intend to take direct military action against the Houthis.** Read against the ~100 US advisers already arming and advising Riyadh's own campaign, this is the clearest marker yet of where the American line sits on the Yemen ground war: support without engaging, even when a close partner asks by name at the moment its own coastal position collapses. Attributed to unnamed US officials in a liveblog; no on-record DoD or White House statement located. ([France24](https://www.france24.com/en/middle-east/20260911-middle-east-live-trump-says-he-has-no-regrets-about-launching-war-on-iran))

## 2026-09-10 — Houthis push past Mokha to reach the Hanish Islands, tightening the squeeze on Bab el-Mandeb

- **Houthi forces pushed past Wednesday's capture of the port of Mokha (already on this thread's 09-09 record) to reach the Hanish Islands in the Red Sea, tightening pressure on the Bab el-Mandeb strait.** Maritime Executive frames the advance as accelerating toward the group's stated goal of controlling the coastline down to the peninsula overlooking Perim Island and Bab el-Mandeb itself. The internationally recognised government still holds the coastline and the Red Sea islands overlooking the shipping channels in this specific area, as of that report. The Houthis deny that blocking traffic through the strait is the objective, saying they can already control it from positions they hold now — their denial is carried here, not adopted. ([Maritime Executive](https://maritime-executive.com/article/houthis-break-through-towards-bab-el-mandeb))
- ⚠️ **A Reuters sourcing claim that Iranian arms and advice helped the Houthis take Mokha could not be opened at Reuters directly in this pass — it is carried here only as attributed to Reuters via secondary pickup, unconfirmed.** For the oil-market read on the same advance (two chokepoints being priced simultaneously), see [Red Sea Shock](/threads/red-sea-oil-shock/).

## 2026-09-09 — Houthis capture the Red Sea port of Mokha as government forces withdraw to Dhubab; two-week toll across five fronts reaches 194 dead

- **Houthi forces seized the strategic Red Sea port city of Mokha on 09-09 after days of fighting, as pro-government forces withdrew roughly 46km south to Dhubab — a major reversal from this thread's 09-04 record of government forces claiming to hold the hills overlooking the port.** Mokha sits just north of the Bab al-Mandeb strait; capturing it gives the Houthis new positions to threaten shipping through the strait. Houthi fighters were reported chanting "Death to Israel" while taking the city. ([The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/mena/2026/09/10/houthis-yemen-mokha-port-red-sea/), [The Media Line](https://themedialine.org/headlines/houthis-take-mokha-as-government-forces-withdraw-from-red-sea-port/), [Times of Israel liveblog](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog-september-10-2026))
- **Al Jazeera's 09-10 roundup puts the two-week toll across five active fronts (Taiz, the western coast/Mokha, al-Jawf, Marib, al-Dhalea) at 194 killed and 880 wounded, per aid agencies** — a wider geographic spread than this thread's prior entries, which had tracked Taiz/Mokha/Hodeidah specifically. Al-Dhalea is newly named as a front, described as the gateway between Houthi territory and the southern governorates. ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/10/houthis-yemeni-forces-engaged-in-fierce-battles-why-each-front-matters))

## 2026-09-08 — Houthi strikes on Aramco and four Saudi cities mark the sharpest escalation since the 2022 truce

- **Houthi drones and ballistic missiles struck Saudi Aramco energy facilities and four Saudi cities overnight, wounding at least 73 including women and children — the sharpest Yemen-Saudi escalation since the 2022 truce.** Houthi military spokesman Yahya Saree claimed strikes on energy infrastructure in southern Saudi Arabia including near the 400,000-barrel-a-day Jizan refinery; coalition spokesman Maj-Gen Turki al-Malki said Abha, Khamis Mushait, Jazan and Najran were hit. It follows Saudi air and cruise-missile strikes on Houthi-held al-Jawf, al-Bayda, Marib and Taiz — including a strike on al-Hazm prison the Houthis say killed seven — and Yemeni government counteroffensives begun Monday. ⚠️ The 73-wounded figure traces to a single Saudi official statement repeated by several outlets, not to independent counts. ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/8/saudi-houthi-fighting-in-yemen-escalates-what-happened-and-whats-next))

## 2026-09-07 — A third strike on Jizan in three months as the Yemen front reaches Saudi oil infrastructure

- **The 09-07 strike on Aramco's Jizan refinery is the third on that facility since fighting between Saudi Arabia and the Houthis resumed this summer — after the July Jizan and Yanbu drone attacks and the 08-09 strike that started a fire the Saudi Energy Ministry said was extinguished with no injuries.** No group had claimed the latest attack at this cut, but the pattern is the Yemen war reaching across the border into Saudi export infrastructure rather than staying on the Red Sea shipping lane. ([OilPrice](https://oilprice.com/Latest-Energy-News/World-News/Saudi-Aramcos-Jizan-Refinery-Hit-Again-as-Houthi-Attacks-Escalate.html), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/07/oil-prices-rise-to-6-week-high-after-iran-and-us-trade-blows-saudi-aramco-facilities-reportedly-hit.html))
- **Yemen's government military said late Sunday its warplanes struck Houthi positions in al-Barah (south of Hodeidah), the western outskirts of Marib, al-Lubnat, al-Alam and al-Jawf governorate centre, claiming heavy Houthi losses, while Houthi military spokesman Yahya Saree said Monday Houthi forces shot down three Saudi drones — a CH-4 over al-Jawf and a Wing Loong II over al-Bayda — in the past 24 hours.** No Saudi comment on the drone claims. The government air campaign is a new escalation beyond the ground fighting already on this thread; the fighting continues over control of the Bab al-Mandab corridor. ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/7/yemen-govt-forces-launch-air-strikes-on-houthi-targets))

## 2026-09-06 — A third day of Mokha-front fighting: government forces claim Hais district, Houthi shelling kills four civilians on Taiz-Aden road

- **Yemen's military said Sunday it recaptured Hais district south of Hodeidah — a district linking the Hodeidah, Taiz and Ibb provinces — and captured "dozens" of Houthi fighters in al-Jarahi district, while Houthi shelling on the Taiz-Aden road killed four civilians and wounded nine, and clashes in Taiz itself killed more than 60 on Saturday alone (already logged 09-05).** The UN's OCHA said about 1,800 families have been displaced over the two days of fighting. The Yemeni government warned any Houthi movement toward Bab al-Mandab would face "harsh attrition." A field officer in Taiz called the "coming hours... decisive" as reinforcements arrived from Aden. AFP's running tally puts the two-day toll at "nearly 200." No statement found this window from the UN envoy on a formal truce collapse. ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/6/yemeni-forces-claim-strategic-district-amid-intensified-houthi-clashes))

## 2026-09-05 — A second day of the heaviest fighting in years: more than sixty killed, including six civilians in a bus strike in Taiz

- **More than sixty people were killed on 09-05 in fighting between Houthi forces and Saudi-backed government forces — 26 government troops, 31 Houthi fighters and six civilians, the last in a missile strike on a bus in Taiz** — on the second day of a Houthi western-coast offensive that has pushed toward the port of Mokha and the hills overlooking the Bab al-Mandab strait. AFP's wire carried the identical breakdown later on Saturday, so the count is now wire-corroborated rather than single-outlet. ([Times of Israel liveblog](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog-september-05-2026/), [AFP via Aaj English TV](https://english.aaj.tv/news/330471026/fresh-yemen-clashes-kill-more-than-60-military-medical-sources))

## 2026-09-04 — A Houthi ground offensive toward Mokha and Bab al-Mandab kills more than 120 in two days, the worst clashes in years

- **A Houthi ground offensive launched 09-03/04 in western Yemen pushed toward the Red Sea port of Mokha and the hills overlooking the Bab al-Mandab strait, with the toll from Thursday climbing past 120 (AFP, Arab News) and to 129 (Gulf News) by Friday, including a missile strike on a restaurant in al-Makha with civilian casualties.** Government forces claimed the same day to have recaptured some of the hills; Houthi officials renewed threats to close Bab al-Mandab if Gulf states join the wider war. The heaviest fighting inside Yemen since the 2022 truce, and the front the UN's envoy warned in August could return the country to full-scale war. ([Arab News, AFP](https://www.arabnews.com/middle-east/more-than-120-killed-in-yemen-clashes-since-thursday-sources-3000492), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/4/yemens-army-says-recaptured-key-territory-as-clashes-with-houthis-continue), [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/yemen/yemen-sees-worst-clashes-in-years-as-fighting-kills-129-1.500664105))
