## 2026-06-09 — the EU blocks the launch; the real regulatory angle

- **Apple withheld Siri AI from EU iPhones at iOS 27 launch, citing DMA
  compliance** — EU regulators publicly rejected Apple's exemption
  request ("no tech rule exemption"); ~450M EU iPhone users affected.
  This is the actual antitrust/regulatory development in the dark window,
  distinct from (and better-sourced than) the flagged "cloud extension."

## 2026-01-12 — official deal confirmed, terms partially disclosed

- **Google and Apple jointly confirm a multi-year Gemini partnership**:
  "the next generation of Apple Foundation Models will be based on
  Google's Gemini models and cloud technology," with hybrid
  on-device/Private Cloud Compute processing explicitly preserved.
  **No financial terms or exclusivity disclosed officially** — the
  reported ~$1B/yr figure (Bloomberg, 2025-11-05) has never been
  confirmed by either company.
  ([Google's joint statement](https://blog.google/company-news/inside-google/company-announcements/joint-statement-google-apple/))
