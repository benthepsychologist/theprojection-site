## 2026-08-13 — AMD prices its biggest-ever dollar bond sale to fund the buildout

- **AMD launched a four-part senior unsecured bond offering targeting
  $4-5B (notes due 2029/2031/2033/2036), ultimately pricing at $4.75B —
  its largest-ever US dollar debt raise.** Proceeds are for general
  corporate purposes including possible debt repayment, as AMD funds its
  expansion into the AI/data-center compute this thread already tracks
  (the ~14GW of OpenAI/Meta/Anthropic deals, above). Bank of America,
  JPMorgan, Barclays and Wells Fargo led; bonds settle 08-17. First time
  this thread has seen AMD reach for public debt rather than cash/equity
  to fund the buildout — a leverage signal worth watching alongside the
  hardware-delivery timeline.
  ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-13/amd-plans-to-raise-as-much-as-5-billion-from-debt-offering))

*Watch:* Whether the ~14GW of announced OpenAI/Meta/Anthropic compute
deals actually convert to shipped hardware and vested warrants on their
stated H2-2026/H1-2027 timelines, or stay a backlog/announcement story —
tonight's Q2 2026 earnings (due after this crawl closed) is the first
real test. Also watching: China MI325X licensing status, and whether the
Oracle 50,000-GPU Q3-2026 rollout starts on schedule.

## 2026-08-06→07 — AMD buys a model-specific-silicon startup

- **AMD acquired Taalas, a Toronto startup that hardwires specific AI
  models directly into silicon** — its HC1 chip encodes Llama 3.1 8B
  across 53B transistors, quoted at ~17,000 tokens/sec/user at ~200W;
  Taalas says it can go from design to finished silicon in about two
  months. AMD plans to fold the approach into its Instinct accelerator
  roadmap as a differentiated-inference play; terms undisclosed. Announced
  08-06, still leading AI-newsletter coverage into 08-07.
  ([AMD Newsroom](https://newsroom.amd.com/news/amd-acquires-taalas-ai-inference/), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/06/amd-buys-taalas-startup-that-hardwires-ai-models-into-its-silicon.html))

## 2026-08-05 — Musk declares SpaceX/xAI "exclusive to Nvidia"; Wall Street re-rates AMD upward anyway

- **On SpaceX's own earnings call, Elon Musk said SpaceX and xAI are now
  "exclusive to Nvidia"** for future AI infrastructure — "we've decided
  to build exclusively on Nvidia because we think the Vera Rubin
  architecture is the best architecture" — tied to a joint SpaceX/Nvidia
  compute payload for the **Starmind AI1** orbital AI satellite. He
  didn't name AMD, but coverage reads it as closing off SpaceX/xAI as a
  future AMD customer, a competitive-narrative headwind landing the day
  after AMD's Q2 beat. SpaceX itself reported $7.8B revenue (+92% YoY)
  but a $541M loss, and fell over 7% after-hours.
  ([BNN Bloomberg](https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/video/shows/the-open/2026/08/05/musk-says-spacex-will-use-nvidias-chips-not-amds/) ·
  [dnyuz](https://dnyuz.com/2026/08/05/spacex-and-nvidia-are-taking-their-relationship-exclusive-heres-what-musk-said-about-their-new-status/))
- **Wall Street re-rated AMD upward despite the ~9% post-earnings drop**,
  reading it as a valuation reset rather than a fundamentals miss: Truist
  raised its target from $478 to $594 (Buy), citing data-center revenue
  expected to double by 2027; Bernstein raised $600 to $650 (Outperform),
  citing the ~$13B Q3 guide beating consensus with data center projected
  at $8.1B vs. Street's $7.7B; KeyBanc reaffirmed $725 (Overweight),
  citing AI GPU revenue of ~$2.9B, +190% YoY.
  ([Finbold](https://finbold.com/analysts-update-amd-stock-price-target-2/))

## 2026-08-04 — Q2 beats and raises; the stock falls anyway

- **Double beat, sold off ~8-9% after-hours on valuation/margin
  concerns.** Revenue $11.536B (record, +50% YoY, above the $11.2B ±
  $300M guide); Data Center $6.718B (+107% YoY, above the ~$6.5B
  consensus); Q3 guide ~$13B. The Anthropic MI450/Helios partnership (up
  to 2GW) reaffirmed, first gigawatt now slated H1 2027 (not H2 2026 as
  earlier framed); Helios rack shipments named OpenAI, Meta, Microsoft,
  Oracle and Anthropic among recipients, shipments beginning later in Q3
  2026. Coverage attributes the sell-off to stretched valuation (above
  Nvidia's own multiples) and flat 56% margin guidance — Wall Street
  wanted a blowout Q3 forecast, not an in-line beat.
  ([AMD IR](https://ir.amd.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1295/amd-reports-second-quarter-2026-financial-results))

### 2026-08-04 — Q2 2026 earnings due tonight, pipeline still unproven in revenue

- **AMD reports Q2 2026 earnings today after market close** (5:00pm ET
  call). AMD's own guide from Q1: **~$11.2B revenue ± $300M**; Street
  consensus **~$11.3B** (+46-47% YoY), **~$1.61 non-GAAP EPS**, Data
  Center segment estimated near **$6.5B**. As of this crawl, no 8-K with
  results had been filed on SEC EDGAR — the print itself falls after this
  crawl closed and is unresolved here.

### 2026-07-22/23 — Advancing AI 2026: MI400/Helios hit volume production, Anthropic signs

- **AMD's Advancing AI 2026 event** (San Francisco): Lisa Su presented
  the first volume-production details of the **MI400 accelerator
  family** and the **Helios rack-scale platform** (72x Instinct MI455X
  GPUs per rack, priced $5.0-5.5M/rack), plus 6th-gen **EPYC Venice**
  CPUs.
- **Same window, AMD and Anthropic announced a strategic partnership**:
  Anthropic to deploy up to **2GW of AMD Instinct MI450 Series GPUs**
  (first GW starting H1 2027), plus an AMD **equity investment of up to
  $5B** in Anthropic tied to deployment milestones, plus a joint
  ROCm/Claude engineering collaboration. Unlike the OpenAI/Meta deals,
  this is real equity, not a warrant — it already dominates AMD's
  board-tracked thrust axis (~$5.3B/yr). Diversifies Anthropic's compute
  supply beyond Google TPU/Cloud; gives AMD its second marquee
  frontier-lab customer after OpenAI.
  ([AMD IR](https://ir.amd.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1292/amd-and-anthropic-announce-strategic-partnership-to-deploy-up-to-2-gigawatts-of-amd-instinct-mi450-series-gpus) ·
  [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/22/amd-anthropic-ai-chip-investment.html))
- **Microsoft Azure confirmed the same event** it will deploy Helios
  racks, with delivery expected before end of 2026 for frontier-model
  inference and Azure AI services.
  ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/20/amd-helios-microsoft-ai-nvidia.html))
- **Oracle Cloud** committed to a **50,000-GPU rollout starting Q3
  2026** (expansion of a partnership originally announced 2025-10-14);
  India's **Tata Consultancy Services** also named as a deployment
  partner. Coverage described **OpenAI's + Meta's commitments combined
  as 12GW**, putting AMD's total disclosed forward pipeline (with
  Anthropic's 2GW) near **14GW**.

### 2026-01 — China tightens: MI325X moves to case-by-case licensing

- **A new case-by-case US licensing regime began**, requiring individual
  approval for **MI325X** shipments to Chinese buyers — tighter than the
  MI308 revenue-share arrangement below. Lisa Su has said China remains
  **~20% of AMD revenue** despite the restrictions (single-source
  figure, not independently corroborated this crawl).
  ([cryptobriefing.com](https://cryptobriefing.com/amd-china-revenue-export-restrictions/))

### 2026-02-24 — Meta signs a second 6GW deal, days after expanding with Nvidia

- **AMD and Meta announced an expanded partnership**: up to **6GW of
  AMD Instinct GPUs**, days after Meta had also committed to millions of
  Nvidia GPUs — outlets framed it as Meta breaking Nvidia's de facto
  exclusivity. First-gigawatt shipments (MI450-based GPU + EPYC Venice
  CPU, Helios architecture, ROCm) scheduled to begin **H2 2026**. AMD
  issued Meta a second performance-based warrant for **up to 160 million
  shares**, vesting on the same three-part structure as OpenAI's (below):
  shipment tranches, AMD share-price thresholds, and Meta milestones.
  ([AMD IR](https://ir.amd.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1279/amd-and-meta-announce-expanded-strategic-partnership-to-deploy-6-gigawatts-of-amd-gpus) ·
  [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/02/24/meta-to-use-6gw-of-amd-gpus-days-after-expanded-nvidia-ai-chip-deal.html))

### 2025-10-27 — ZT Systems manufacturing arm divested to Sanmina, design team kept

- **The Sanmina divestiture of ZT Systems' US data-center manufacturing
  business closed** (~$3B cash+stock incl. up to $450M contingent,
  announced May 2025) — AMD retained ZT's rack-scale AI-solutions
  design and customer-enablement teams, the group doing the Helios
  integration work described above. (The underlying $4.9B ZT Systems
  acquisition itself had closed March 2025.)
  ([AMD Newsroom](https://newsroom.amd.com/news/amd-completes-divestiture-of-zt-systems-data-cente/))

### 2025-10-06 — OpenAI opens the playbook: 6GW, warrant to $600/share

- **AMD and OpenAI announced a strategic partnership**: up to **6GW of
  AMD Instinct GPUs** across multiple hardware generations, first 1GW
  (MI450-based) targeted for H2 2026. **AMD stock jumped ~23%** same
  day. AMD issued OpenAI a warrant for **up to 160 million shares**
  (~10% of shares outstanding), $0.01 exercise price, vesting on
  shipment tranches (1-6GW) plus AMD share-price thresholds plus OpenAI
  milestones — **the final tranche requires AMD stock at $600/share**;
  warrants run roughly five years, to October 2030. As of AMD's FY2025
  10-K/A (year ended Dec 27 2025), **none of these warrant shares had
  vested** — zero financial-statement impact through year-end 2025.
  ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/10/06/openai-amd-chip-deal-ai.html) ·
  [Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/gpus/openai-signs-6gw-amd-gpu-deal) ·
  [SEC EDGAR — 10-K/A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2488/000000248826000021/amd-20251227.htm))

### 2025-08 — China MI308 restrictions hit revenue, then resume under a revenue-share

- **AMD disclosed an estimated ~$800M revenue hit** from US export
  restrictions on the China-specific **MI308** accelerator. MI308
  exports then **resumed** under a conditions-based arrangement
  requiring AMD to share China-sales revenue with the US government —
  the same mechanism later reported for Nvidia's H20.
  ([qz.com](https://qz.com/amd-lisa-su-china-export-restrictions-commerce-department) ·
  [ainvest.com](https://www.ainvest.com/news/amd-resumes-mi308-ai-chip-exports-china-trump-administration-reverses-policy-2507/))
