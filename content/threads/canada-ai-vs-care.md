## 2026-07-23 → 07-31 — The 2027 cliff gets a month and a standoff

*Logged 2026-08-18, nineteen days late.*

- **The bilateral health funding agreements lapse in March 2027**, per the
  Charlottetown First Ministers' communiqué — refining this thread's prior
  "lapsing 2027, unreplaced" to an actual month. The mental-health and
  substance-use stream is inside that lapse.
  ([CPAC](https://www.cpac.ca/headline-politics/episode/pm-carney-and-premiers-on-us-tariff-threat-health-care--july-23-2026))
- **Premiers pressed PM Carney directly for renewal certainty at the
  07-23 meeting, and he did not commit** — punting to a future
  finance-and-health-ministers meeting **with no date set.** This is the
  thread's central question moving from a budget-document absence into an
  on-the-record federal–provincial standoff.
- Follow-on coverage through 07-27/07-31 has premiers describing a coming
  **"fiscal cliff"** and advocates publicly urging Ottawa to renew, with
  the federal government still uncommitted as of 2026-08-18.
- **Nothing new on the AI side of the ledger.** Amii's $10M Health
  Innovation Lab, Ontario's hallucinating-scribes audit and the Santé
  Québec pilot all resurfaced in generic coverage this window but all
  pre-date 2026-07-25 as events. **The asymmetry this thread names — AI
  infrastructure funded, mental-health funding unrenewed — is unchanged.**


## 2026-07-28 — Private AI-health capital keeps outpacing public MH money

- **French insurtech Alan (first new Canadian health insurer since
  1957) is scaling its AI-powered care-navigation model in Canada** —
  a $780M Series G ($8.9B valuation) funds expansion beyond its ~4,100
  members/100 employers, with an AI health assistant slated for launch
  next year. The counter-signal keeps recurring: private AI-health
  capital moves fast in Canada while the federal MH funding side (the
  2027 bilateral-agreement cliff) stays static.
  ([The Logic](https://thelogic.co/news/alan-insurance-expansion-ai/))
