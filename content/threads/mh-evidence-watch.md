## 2026-09-24 — A Phase 3 of oral ketamine for depression and a VA art-therapy PTSD trial are registered

- **India's JIPMER registered a Phase 3 comparing oral ketamine with oral midazolam as the comparator for major depressive episodes (first posted 09-24).** 220 participants are planned, randomized and triple-masked, with a start estimated for October and the Hamilton Depression Rating Scale as primary outcome; the sponsor frames it as a test of an affordable, scalable alternative to esketamine. Registration only, no data. ([ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07839338](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07839338))
- **The VA Office of Research and Development registered ART-VET, a remotely delivered art-therapy treatment for veterans with PTSD tested against a waitlist control (first posted 09-24).** 84 participants are planned with a start estimated 10-01 and the clinician-rated CAPS-5 as primary outcome. Registration only, no data. ([ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07840079](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07840079))
- **Acadia Pharmaceuticals' remlifanserin narrowly missed the primary endpoint of its Phase 2 trial in Alzheimer's-disease psychosis on 09-24 (p=0.0603) while beating placebo on a key secondary measure, and the company will continue Phase 3 with the 30 mg arm dropped.** On the hallucinations-and-delusions scale (SAPS-H+D) the 60 mg dose fell 12.6 points against 10.4 for placebo at week 6 (effect size 0.26); on the clinician global severity scale (CGI-S-ADP) it fell 1.3 against 0.9 (effect size 0.37, nominal p=0.0077), and the 30 mg dose did little. Acadia reports adverse events similar to placebo and no QT signal, will present details at CTAD in Boston on 11-16 to 11-19, said it is not proposing a change to the Phase 3 primary endpoint, and saw shares slide nearly 10% at the open (BioSpace); Acadia notes no drug is FDA-approved for the condition. ([Acadia press release](https://acadia.com/en-us/media/news-releases/acadia-pharmaceuticals-announces-phase-3-enabling-topline-results-from-phase-2-radiant-study-of-remlifanserin-for-the-treatment-of-alzheimers-disease-psychosis-adp), [BioSpace](https://www.biospace.com/acadia-slides-on-phase-2-miss-in-alzheimers-psychosis-but-keeps-moving-asset-forward))

## 2026-09-23 — A randomized test of a Soteria-style inpatient unit for acute psychosis is registered

- **A Jerusalem Mental Health Center randomized trial compares a closed psychiatric ward built around Soteria principles with a conventional closed ward for adults in acute psychosis.** 200 participants are planned, enrolling since September 2025; outcomes include psychotic symptom severity, therapeutic alliance, one-year readmission and antipsychotic dose. ([ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07836348](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07836348))
- **The CDC's National Center for Health Statistics reported that the final US suicide rate for 2024 was 13.7 per 100,000, down from 14.1 in 2023, with no significant change since 2017.** The decline was concentrated in women 25 and older and in males 15 to 24 (21.2 to 20.2) and 25 to 44 (29.8 to 28.5); firearms remained the leading means for both sexes with no significant change, state rates ran from 5.7 in the District of Columbia to 29.7 in Alaska, and the rate for females 10 to 14 was 2.3 against 2.1 in 2023. ([CDC NCHS Data Brief 572](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/data/databriefs/db572.pdf))

## 2026-09-18 — A Swedish trial of a smartphone game for residual depression, funded by the game's maker

- **A Swedish open-label randomized trial of 148 adults on antidepressants with lingering depressive symptoms found that a smartphone game plus optional internet CBT modules and on-demand therapist support cut self-rated depression scores 5.06 points more than a waitlist over nine weeks, in a study funded by the game's maker.** As Medscape summarizes the paper (Journal of Affective Disorders, online 09-18, Linköping University), 51.1% of the intervention group reached at least a 30% improvement against 18.6% of controls, anxiety scores fell 2.95 points more and self-reported medication adherence rose, but quality of life did not differ; about 41% of the intervention arm gave no post-treatment depression data, the package cannot separate the game from the CBT modules and therapist support, recruitment was through social media and mostly women, and the maker of the game (Fig) funded it. Medscape's coverage ran 09-24. ([Medscape](https://www.medscape.com/viewarticle/add-serious-smartphone-game-based-therapy-linked-reduced-2026a1000zn3))

## 2026-09-17 — Xenon pauses new enrollment in its Phase 3 depression trials after psychosis events

- **Xenon Pharmaceuticals paused enrollment in its Phase 3 depression trials of azetukalner on 09-17 after adverse events that included psychosis, and said it will end the major-depression trial early with topline data due in the first quarter of 2027.** The company's chief medical officer told analysts a very small number of patients had events coded under the broad term "psychosis"; existing participants stay on drug in X-Nova2 (major depression, about 360 enrolled, roughly 80% of target) and X-Ceed (bipolar depression), and Xenon plans to adjust dosing over the coming months and has notified the FDA. It filed for FDA approval of azetukalner in focal seizures the same week, where each Phase 3 trial had one psychosis case; shares fell 26% premarket on 09-18 from a $57.35 close, and William Blair cut its estimate of the chance of success in major depression from 60% to 50%. ([Fierce Biotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/xenon-pauses-phase-3-depression-trials-after-psychosis-events), [The Catalyst Brief](https://www.thecatalystbrief.com/article/xenon-pauses-phase-3-depression-enrollment-after-psychosis-events-while-keeping-existing-patients-on-drug))

## 2026-09-11 — A 2,477-person Swedish trial finds format, not modality, is the constraint on unguided internet therapy; a small Korean trial and a wire-sourced suicide-epidemiology finding round out the day

- **A Swedish factorial RCT of 2,477 people found unguided internet-delivered CBT and unguided internet-delivered psychodynamic therapy equally effective for depression and anxiety (d = 0.38–0.65 against waitlist), gains holding at 24 months — but participants completed fewer than half the treatment modules on average, extending treatment from 8 to 16 weeks added nothing, and peer-forum access added no benefit.** Lindqvist, Mechler, Hlynsson, Andersson and Carlbring, *Internet Interventions*, a 3×2×2 design crossing treatment arm (Unified Protocol CBT, affect-focused psychodynamic therapy, waitlist) with duration (8 vs 16 weeks) and peer-forum access. Head to head, the two modalities did not differ. This is this thread's anchor finding restated with a large trial: the format's adherence ceiling, not the treatment model inside it, is what's binding — evidence that expands understanding of reach and dropout, not of efficacy beyond what's already established.
  ([PubMed, PMID 42633284](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42633284/), DOI 10.1016/j.invent.2026.100980, trial NCT05016843)
- **A small open-label Korean RCT (N=52) found a guided mobile CBT app for panic disorder beat structured psychoeducation on clinician-rated panic severity (least-squares mean difference −3.71, P=.02) but on no self-reported outcome measure.** Kang et al., *Internet Interventions*, online 2026-07-09; 21 of 26 (80.8%) completed, no serious adverse events. The authors themselves flag the open-label, unblinded-rater design as limiting — a real but modest result, not independent replication of anything larger.
  ([PubMed, PMID 42473578](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42473578/))
- **A JAMA Pediatrics study found the US suicide rate among girls aged 10–14 more than quadrupled between 2007 and 2024, from 0.51 to 2.28 per 100,000, closing what used to be a wide gender gap.** Boys' rate in the same age band fell 35% from 2018 to 2024 while girls' rose 13%, leaving boys only 4% higher than girls by 2024 against nearly double in 2017; the narrowing held across Black, Hispanic and White children in the sample. Suicide is the second-leading cause of death for US 10–14-year-olds. ⚠️ Verified through ten independent wire-affiliate pickups citing JAMA Pediatrics, not by opening the paper itself — the design and data source are not independently confirmed here.

## 2026-08-11 — FACE app RCT: another "reach vs. efficacy" data point, this time for prevention

- **A randomised controlled trial of the FACE app — a guided, two-component
  digital selective-prevention program for emerging adults with a history
  of adverse childhood experiences — found its primary well-being outcomes
  null against care-as-usual, but its more targeted proximal mechanisms
  (self-efficacy for managing emotions, fear of negative evaluation,
  negative problem orientation) moved favorably.** 167 of 1,514 eligible
  people enrolled (11% uptake) from a population-based Swiss cohort; the
  authors' own read is that limited uptake in unselected high-risk
  populations, not efficacy, is the bigger open problem for this kind of
  tool. Fits this thread's running pattern: digital tools extend reach and
  move specific mechanisms, but broad outcome measures (well-being,
  resilience) aren't moving with them.
  ([DOI: 10.1016/j.invent.2026.100987](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.invent.2026.100987), *Internet Interventions*)

## 2026-02 — Cuijpers state-of-the-art review: progress is cumulative, not transformative

- **Fewer than 40% of people with depression, anxiety, or substance-use
  disorder receive any treatment at all**, and in low- and middle-income
  countries only 1 in 27 people with depression gets adequate care, per
  Pim Cuijpers' February 2026 *World Psychiatry* state-of-the-art review.
  It frames four innovation tracks — digital, personalized/precision, new
  therapies (psychedelics, transdiagnostic, cognitive bias modification),
  and dissemination (task-sharing, digital) — and concludes progress
  across all of them has been incremental, not transformative. Paywalled
  at crawl time; summarized via secondary coverage.
  ([Wiley/World Psychiatry](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/wps.70002))

## 2024-06 — Cuijpers meta-analysis: absolute response rates run 24-42% across eight disorders

- **The anchor finding for this whole strand: therapy clearly beats
  control, but absolute response is modest.** Cuijpers et al.'s June 2024
  *World Psychiatry* meta-analysis (441 RCTs, n=33,881) found response
  rates (≥50% symptom reduction) of only 42% for major depression, 38%
  for PTSD/OCD/panic disorder, 36% for GAD, 32% for social
  anxiety/specific phobia, down to 24% for borderline personality
  disorder — meaning most patients in "successful" trials still don't
  reach a strong response. This is the "reach vs. efficacy" frame this
  strand keeps returning to: most of what's new in the field (task-sharing,
  single-session interventions, digital-adjacent delivery) expands who
  gets treatment, not how well treatment works once they get it.
  ([PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38727072/))
