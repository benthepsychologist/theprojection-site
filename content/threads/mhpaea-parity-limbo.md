- **The administration announced it will propose a REPLACEMENT for the
  2024 MHPAEA final rule** — which has been non-enforced since May 2025 —
  with a reported "by end of year" target (thin, single source). The
  operative fact: no payer compliance deadline currently exists; parity
  is a rule-making vacuum, not a countdown.
