## 2026-08-07 — A related but distinct legal action: NM court orders Meta to pay $567M over child mental-health harm

- **A New Mexico judge (Bryan Biedscheid) ordered Meta to pay $567M into a
  child mental-health abatement fund, calling the company a "public
  nuisance,"** in NM Attorney General Raúl Torrez's algorithmic-design
  suit (filed 2023) — on top of the $375M in civil penalties a jury
  ordered against Meta in March 2026 after finding it knowingly harmed
  children's mental health and concealed what it knew about child sexual
  exploitation on its platforms. This is a different case and a
  different mechanism than the Wired ad-platform story that opened this
  thread (design/engagement harm vs. AI-generated CSAM in paid ads), but
  the March jury's CSAM-concealment finding underpins both, so it's
  logged here rather than split into a new thread. Ruling landed evening
  of 2026-08-06 ET, ahead of the 2026-08-07 digest window; dated
  explicitly rather than presented as breaking today.
  ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/06/meta-to-pay-into-567-million-fund-after-child-harms-case-new-mexico.html), [PBS NewsHour](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/nation/new-mexico-court-orders-meta-to-pay-567-million-over-mental-health-harms-to-kids-online))
