## 2026-08-17 — Second rolling cohort opens; a parallel FDA pathway appears

- **CMS's ACCESS model opened its next participant cohort today, the
  second rolling start of a 10-year program.** The CMS Innovation
  Center's model page names 2026-08-17 as a scheduled rolling start,
  with the next on October 1. A rolling administrative start generates
  no announcement of its own, so what is confirmed is CMS's pre-stated
  schedule standing unrevised — logged as a hit on that basis and no
  stronger.
  ([CMS Innovation Center](https://www.cms.gov/priorities/innovation/innovation-models/access))
- **A same-day page refresh adds mild corroboration:** CMS's
  accepted-applicants page now carries a "last updated: August 17,
  2026" stamp and lists roughly 280 accepted organizations, up from
  the 150+ cited earlier — consistent with a cohort actually opening,
  though still administrative rather than announced.
  ([CMS accepted applicants](https://www.cms.gov/priorities/innovation/access-model-accepted-applicants))
- **Separately, the FDA named Cadence as the second participant in its
  TEMPO pilot, which is coordinated with CMS innovation models and
  exempts digital health devices from premarket authorisation while
  they collect real-world data through Medicare.** Dexcom was first,
  07-23. Behavioural health is one of four named CMS focus areas with
  no participant yet. Worth tracking alongside ACCESS as a second
  federal route by which behavioural-health technology reaches
  Medicare beneficiaries.
  ([MedTech Dive](https://www.medtechdive.com/news/fda-names-second-participant-in-tempo-digital-health-pilot/828027/))

## 2026-07-23 — The BH track surfaces

- **CMS's ACCESS Model behavioral-health track surfaced** — the 10-year
  Outcome-Aligned-Payments demonstration launched 07-05; ~85 of 150+
  accepted applicants chose the BH track, including Headspace, SonderMind,
  Concert Health, Mindoula Health, and April Health by Wysa — tying
  outcomes-based payment directly to multiple watchlist entities at once.
  ([Fierce Healthcare](https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/digital-health/cms-taps-150-digital-health-companies-providers-access-model))
